A friendly dentist for your child

A good first visit can shape how a child feels about the dentist for life. We keep children’s appointments relaxed and friendly, explain things in words they understand and never rush.

Bring your child for their first check-up when their first tooth appears, or by their first birthday.

Book a visit when

  • Your child’s first tooth appears, or by age one
  • It’s been six months since their last check-up
  • They complain of toothache or you see dark spots
  • A tooth has been knocked or chipped
  • You have questions about thumb-sucking or teething

What happens at your appointment

  1. Meet and greet

    A ride in the chair and a look at the tools, so nothing is a surprise.

  2. A gentle check

    Dr Grace counts and checks teeth, gums and how the bite is developing.

  3. Clean and protect

    A gentle clean, with fluoride where it’s helpful.

  4. Parent chat

    Practical tips on brushing, snacks and what to expect next.

Healthy habits at home

  • Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste: a smear for under-threes, a pea-sized amount from three to six
  • Help with brushing until your child can do it well, usually around seven or eight
  • Keep sweet drinks and snacks to mealtimes
  • Don’t put your child to bed with a bottle of milk or juice

Questions

Do baby teeth really matter?

Yes. They help children eat and speak, and they hold space for adult teeth. Decay in baby teeth can cause pain and infection.

My child is scared. What should I do?

Keep the visit low-key, avoid words like ‘hurt’ or ‘needle’, and tell us beforehand. We’ll go at your child’s pace.

Can I stay with my child?

Of course. Parents are welcome in the room.

How often should you see the dentist?

Why a six-monthly check-up saves you pain and money later, and when you might need to come more often.

Most adults do well with a check-up and professional clean every six months. Some people need to come more often, and a few can come less. Here’s why it matters, and how to tell which group you’re in.

Why regular check-ups are worth it

Tooth decay and gum disease are quiet at first. A small cavity rarely hurts, and early gum disease often only shows as a little bleeding when you brush. By the time something aches, the problem is usually bigger, and so is the treatment.

At a check-up, small problems are found while they’re still quick and affordable to fix. A filling today can save a root canal next year.

What a check-up includes

  • A look at every tooth, your gums and the soft tissues of your mouth
  • X-rays when they’re needed, to see between teeth and below the gum
  • A scale and polish to remove hard build-up (tartar) that brushing can’t shift
  • Honest advice on what’s healthy and what to watch

When you might need to come more often

  • You have gum disease, or your gums bleed often
  • You’ve had a lot of fillings or new decay recently
  • You smoke, or you have diabetes
  • You’re pregnant, when hormones make gums more prone to inflammation
  • You wear braces or have a dry mouth from medication

What about medical aid?

Many medical aid plans cover routine check-ups and cleaning from day-to-day benefits, often twice a year. Plans differ, so it’s worth checking yours. We’re happy to help.

Between visits

  • Brush twice a day for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste
  • Spit, don’t rinse, so the fluoride keeps working
  • Clean between your teeth once a day with floss or interdental brushes
  • Keep sugary drinks and snacks to mealtimes

Not sure when you were last seen? If it’s been more than a year, book a check-up. It’s the easiest way to stay out of pain.

This article is general information, not a diagnosis. For advice about your own teeth, book a check-up.

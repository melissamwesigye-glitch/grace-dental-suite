How Dr Grace works

Dr Grace trained at the University of the Western Cape and opened Grace Dental Suite in Fairfield Estate, Parow, in 2023. It’s proudly a Black woman-owned practice, built with passion, faith, hard work and the trust of every patient who walks through the door, around one idea: going to the dentist shouldn’t be something you dread.

Before any treatment, she talks you through what will happen, what you’ll feel and what it will cost. Nothing starts until you’re comfortable, and you can ask to pause at any time.

Her patients often say it’s the first time a dental visit felt smooth and painless. That comes from careful technique, modern equipment and simply taking the time to listen.

She is best known for composite bonding: rebuilding chipped, worn, gapped or gold-restored teeth by hand in tooth-coloured resin. She takes her time, typically 2 to 3 hours per tooth, and always aims to save natural teeth wherever possible.

Away from the chair, you’ll find Dr Grace sharing honest, everyday dental advice on TikTok and Instagram.

How Dr Grace works

Dr Grace opened Grace Dental Suite in Fairfield Estate, Parow, in 2023. It is a proudly Black woman-owned practice, built, in her words, with passion, faith, hard work and the trust of the patients who walk through the door.

Before any treatment, she talks you through what will happen. Nothing starts until you’re ready, and you can ask to pause at any time. Patients often describe their visits as smooth and painless.

Much of her work is composite bonding: rebuilding chipped, worn, decayed or gapped teeth, and replacing old gold restorations, by hand in tooth-coloured resin. She typically spends 2 to 3 hours per tooth and aims to save natural teeth wherever possible.

She also shares her cases and everyday dental advice on TikTok and Instagram.

Your first visit

New to Grace Dental Suite? Here’s what to expect, what to bring and how medical aid and payment work.

Book on WhatsAppCall 073 366 1421

How your first visit works

  1. Book

    Send a WhatsApp, call us, or use the form on our contact page. Tell us if you’re nervous or in pain.

  2. Arrive a few minutes early

    We’ll take your details, medical history and medical aid information.

  3. Exam and chat

    Dr Grace examines your teeth and gums, with X-rays if needed, and explains what she finds.

  4. Your plan

    You get your options and costs before treatment, and choose what happens next.

What to bring

  • Your ID or passport
  • Your medical aid card, if you have one
  • A list of any medicines you take, and any allergies
  • Recent dental X-rays or records, if you have them

Medical aid and payment

We’re on the BP Medical Aid Dental Network, so BP Medical Aid members can be treated as network patients.

Patients on other medical aids and those paying cash are welcome too. Benefits differ from plan to plan, so send us your medical aid details before your visit and we’ll help you understand what’s covered.

Dr Grace always explains costs before treatment. If anything isn’t covered, you’ll know before we start.

Nervous about the dentist?

You’re in good company. Many of our patients were, too.

Tell us when you book, and we’ll plan a little more time. Dr Grace will explain each step before she starts, check in with you as she goes, and stop whenever you raise your hand.

If something worries you, whether it’s needles, the sound of the drill or a bad experience in the past, just say so. Knowing helps us make the visit easier for you.

Questions

Do I need to be on BP Medical Aid?

No. We’re on the BP Medical Aid Dental Network, and we also welcome patients on other medical aids and those paying cash.

Can I get a quote before treatment?

Yes. Dr Grace explains your treatment options and what they cost before anything is done, so there are no surprises.

What if I’m nervous about the dentist?

You’re not alone. Tell us when you book. We’ll explain each step before we start, go at your pace, and you can ask to pause at any time.

How do I book?

Send us a WhatsApp, call the practice, or use the booking form on our contact page.

Dental check-ups and cleaning in Parow

A check-up is a short, unhurried visit where Dr Grace looks at your teeth, gums and mouth, and a professional clean removes the hard build-up your toothbrush can’t reach.

Catching small problems early is the easiest way to avoid toothache, bigger treatment and bigger bills later. Most adults do well with a check-up every six months. If you have gum problems or a history of decay, we may suggest coming more often.

Time to book if

  • It’s been more than six months since your last visit
  • Your gums bleed when you brush or floss
  • You have bad breath that doesn’t go away
  • A tooth feels sensitive to hot, cold or sweet things
  • You’re pregnant or planning to be, when healthy gums matter even more

What happens at your appointment

  1. We chat first

    About your health, any medicines you take and anything that’s been bothering you.

  2. A careful exam

    Dr Grace checks every tooth, your gums, your bite and the soft tissues of your mouth, with X-rays if they’re needed.

  3. Scale and polish

    Hard build-up is gently removed above and below the gum line, then your teeth are polished smooth.

  4. Your plan

    You leave knowing what’s healthy, what to watch and what, if anything, needs treatment.

Aftercare

Your gums may feel a little tender for a day after a scale. Keep brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste and clean between your teeth once a day.

Questions

Does a scale and polish hurt?

Most people feel some scraping and vibration but no pain. If your gums are sensitive, tell us and we can go slower or numb the area first.

Will my medical aid cover a check-up?

Most medical aid plans cover routine check-ups and cleaning from day-to-day benefits, often twice a year. Benefits differ from plan to plan, so send us your medical aid details and we’ll help you check.

How long does a check-up take?

Plan for about 30 to 45 minutes, a little longer if X-rays are needed.

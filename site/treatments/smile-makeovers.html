Smile makeovers and bonding

Small changes can make a big difference to how you feel about your smile. Composite bonding uses tooth-coloured material to repair chips, close small gaps and even out uneven edges, usually with little or no drilling.

A smile makeover can combine treatments, such as whitening first and then bonding, planned around what you’d like to change.

Bonding can help with

  • Chipped or worn edges
  • Small gaps between front teeth
  • Teeth of uneven length or shape
  • Stained old fillings on front teeth

What happens at your appointment

  1. Consultation

    Tell us what you’d like to change. We take photos and talk through options and costs.

  2. Whitening first (optional)

    Bonding is matched to your shade and won’t whiten later, so any whitening is done first.

  3. Bonding

    Composite is layered, shaped and polished by hand, often in a single visit.

  4. Review

    We check your bite and the look together and make small refinements.

Aftercare

Avoid biting hard objects such as nails, pens and bottle tops.

Coloured drinks can stain composite over time, and a polish at your check-ups keeps it looking fresh.

Questions

Does bonding damage my teeth?

Bonding usually needs little or no removal of natural tooth, which is one of its biggest advantages.

How long does bonding last?

Typically several years before it needs polishing, repairing or replacing, depending on your habits and bite.

Bonding or veneers?

Bonding is quicker, more affordable and easier to repair. If porcelain veneers would suit your goals better, Dr Grace will explain why and what’s involved.

Professional teeth whitening

Professional whitening lightens the natural colour of your teeth safely, under a dentist’s supervision. It’s the simplest way to brighten your smile without changing the shape of your teeth.

Before whitening, Dr Grace checks that your teeth and gums are healthy, because whitening over untreated decay or gum problems can be painful.

Whitening suits you if

  • Coffee, tea, red wine or smoking have stained your teeth
  • Your teeth have yellowed over time
  • You want a brighter smile before a wedding, graduation or photoshoot

Whitening isn’t recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or while you have untreated decay. It also won’t change the colour of existing fillings or crowns.

What happens at your appointment

  1. Check-up

    We make sure your teeth and gums are healthy and record your starting shade.

  2. Your plan

    Dr Grace recommends the whitening option that suits your teeth, budget and timeline.

  3. Whitening

    Your gums are protected and the whitening gel is applied under supervision.

  4. Keeping it bright

    We show you how to keep your new shade for as long as possible.

Aftercare

A day or two of sensitivity is common and fades on its own; a sensitive toothpaste helps.

For 48 hours, go easy on strongly coloured food and drinks such as coffee, tea, red wine and curry.

Questions

Is professional whitening safe?

Yes, when it’s done under a dentist’s supervision after a check-up. Your gums are protected and the strength is chosen for your teeth.

Why not use shop kits or charcoal?

Shop kits are weaker, and one-size trays can let gel irritate your gums. Charcoal is abrasive and can wear enamel, which can make teeth look more yellow over time.

Will my fillings or crowns whiten too?

No. Whitening only works on natural enamel, so fillings or crowns on front teeth may need replacing afterwards to match.

How long do results last?

Usually from several months to a couple of years. Coffee, tea, red wine and smoking bring stains back faster.

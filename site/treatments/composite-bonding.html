Composite bonding, sculpted by hand

Composite bonding is the treatment Grace Dental Suite is best known for. A tooth-coloured resin is layered, sculpted and polished directly onto your natural teeth to repair chips, close gaps, rebuild worn or decayed edges, cover stains and even out uneven shapes.

Because the resin is added to your tooth, very little or none of your natural tooth usually needs to be removed. Many smiles are finished in a single visit, with no lab and no waiting.

We don’t rush bonding. Each tooth is shade-matched and shaped by hand, and we typically spend 2 to 3 hours on each tooth, paying attention to shape, colour, function and how it suits your face and bite. More complex cases may need two appointments.

Bonding can help with

  • Chipped, cracked or worn front teeth
  • Gaps between teeth, including a natural gap you’d like closed
  • Teeth of uneven length or shape
  • Stains or discolouration that cleaning won’t lift
  • Old gold or silver restorations you’d like replaced with a natural look
  • Adult teeth with enamel defects left by decayed baby teeth

What happens at your appointment

  1. Consultation

    Tell us what you’d like to change. Dr Grace checks your bite and gum health and explains options and costs.

  2. Shade matching

    The resin colour is matched to your natural teeth. Any whitening is done first, because bonding won’t whiten later.

  3. Sculpting

    Decay or old restorations are removed where needed, then the resin is layered and shaped tooth by tooth.

  4. Polish and check

    Each tooth is polished to a natural shine and your bite is checked and refined.

Aftercare

Results are immediate. Avoid biting hard objects such as ice, nails, pens and bottle tops, and don’t use your teeth as tools.

Composite is softer than porcelain, so over time it can stain or chip. Regular check-ups and a professional polish keep it looking its best, and small repairs are simple.

Questions

Does bonding damage my teeth?

In suitable cases very little or no natural tooth needs to be removed, which is one of the biggest advantages of bonding over veneers and crowns.

How long does bonding last?

With good care, bonding lasts for years. How long depends on your bite, habits such as grinding, and keeping up with check-ups.

Can you remove the gold on my front teeth?

In many cases, yes. Old gold or silver restorations can be removed and replaced with tooth-coloured bonding. A consultation shows what’s possible for your teeth.

Bonding, composite veneers or porcelain veneers?

Composite is quicker, more conservative and easier to repair, but it isn’t permanent and can stain or chip over time. Dr Grace will be honest about which option suits your goals.

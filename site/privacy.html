Privacy notice

How Grace Dental Suite handles your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

Grace Dental Suite, 11 Kitchener Street, Fairfield Estate, Parow, Cape Town, 7500. Our Information Officer is Dr Grace Mwizerwa. Contact us on 061 325 0776.

What this website collects

This website does not store any personal information. The booking form prepares a WhatsApp message on your own device; nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and then it is handled under WhatsApp’s own privacy terms.

Embedded content

Some pages show content from Instagram, TikTok and Google Maps. These load only when you scroll to them, and those services may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.

Patient information

Information you share with us as a patient (such as contact details, medical history and medical aid details) is used only to provide your dental care, communicate with you and process medical aid claims. It is stored securely and not shared except where needed for your care, your medical aid, or where the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how it is used. To do so, contact us. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
